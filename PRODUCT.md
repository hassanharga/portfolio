# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leaders evaluating Hassan Mohamed for senior full stack or backend-focused work. They need to understand his focus, experience, selected projects, and contact options without reading a full CV first.

## Product Purpose

Present Hassan's professional experience and product work clearly enough for a visitor to judge role fit, inspect relevant evidence, and start a conversation. The portfolio supports the CV and professional profiles; it does not replace them with unsupported marketing claims.

## Brand Personality

Warm, deliberate, and technically grounded. The experience should feel like a well-used engineering workshop: crafted and confident, without becoming corporate, theatrical, or overly polished.

## Anti-references

- Cold blue-black developer portfolios with neon accents.
- Generic landing pages built from repeated icon, heading, and text cards.
- Dashboards, terminal costumes, fake code windows, or decorative technical metrics.
- Claims about scale, performance, or business impact without supporting evidence.

## Design Principles

- Put work and experience before exhaustive tool lists.
- Use specific project and employment facts instead of invented outcomes.
- Make the primary recruiter path clear: positioning, work, experience, capabilities, contact.
- Keep the visual system distinctive but quieter than the content it supports.
- Prefer editorial lists and deliberate hierarchy over repeated card grids.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable text contrast, semantic structure, and reduced-motion preferences. Do not claim a formal conformance level without completing the corresponding audit.
